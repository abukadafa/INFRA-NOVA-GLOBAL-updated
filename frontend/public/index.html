Who We Are

A multidisciplinary enterprise built on decades of hands-on experience.

Infranova Global Concept Ltd was formally incorporated in June 2025, but our roots in Nigeria's commercial and engineering sectors go back further, through earlier generations of business operations. That history now underpins a structured, registered group focused on long-term value, innovation, and results that stand the test of time.

Our Mission

To deliver integrated, innovative, value-driven solutions across engineering, energy, agriculture, and infrastructure — serving as a dependable bridge between challenges and solutions, locally and globally.

Our Vision

To become a leading African conglomerate, known for transformational solutions, ethical practice, and excellence across every sector we operate in.

Our Clients

Individuals, SMEs, large corporations, and government agencies across Nigeria and beyond.

Our Reach

Headquartered in Abuja, with operations and distribution extending across Nigeria and West Africa.
